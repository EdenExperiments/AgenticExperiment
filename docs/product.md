# Product

One account. Several focused products. One inexpensive suite subscription, with AI as BYOK and/or a small included quota, not unlimited tokens inside £4.99.

On the web that is one app with LifeQuest as the shell. On Apple that is several apps, stack still TBC, sharing the Go API where mockups say it is worth it. See `docs/architecture.md`.

**Job:** help someone practise skills, eat, train, and look after mood, without four other subscriptions and without pretending the app is a clinician, dietitian, or PT.

LifeQuest is the hub. Other products keep their own data. Cross-app XP is later and opt-in. Public pride is **proof** (cleared gates, finished sessions), not a raw level. Rest is not failure. Social, when it exists, is opt-in milestone cards, not a default leaderboard.

AI is a pace-aware coach grounded in *this user’s* logs. Keys stay server-side, encrypted. Never scrape third-party recipe sites for a “stock library.”

## What makes it shine

The suite is not the product. These are.

1. **Proof, not streaks.** A cleared gate with evidence, a closed fast, a finished session, a dated journal line. New products do not have streak counters. A missed Tuesday is absence, not a broken chain.
2. **Rest is a non-event.** No shame state, no rest-day XP, no “you should train today.”
3. **Cook what you have.** Recipes start from the pantry. Empty pantry does not call Claude. We do not scrape a stock library.
4. **One clock you close.** Fasting is one open fast per person. Workout is one in-progress session. Opening is idempotent. Closing produces a receipt.
5. **Mood never becomes points.** MindTrack writes `mh_*` only. No chatbot. No mood score. UK crisis resources on every `/mind` screen.
6. **Unknown stays unknown.** Missing macros and unloaded sets are labelled, not invented.
7. **Atmosphere is play.** Cinematic, horror, and kawaii dress LifeQuest only. Product identity (`nutri-saas`, `workout-forge`, `mental-calm`) stays on the product layout. Horror never touches `/mind`.

Horizon items have homes on the catalog in `docs/architecture.md`. Sleep, meditation, and five focus vibes are on `docs/apps/lifequest.md`, NutriLog features on `docs/apps/nutrilog.md`, and GPS and watches on `docs/apps/workout.md`.
